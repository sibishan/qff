Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 @ UTS · Hackathon briefing

What counts as quantum

Real work, not decoration

Doing real work

  • Measurement outcomes decide what happens next
  • Players pick gates, and the circuit's state changes the game
  • Entanglement links what two players see

Just decorating

  • A circuit runs once at startup, then nothing uses it
  • Ordinary random numbers with a Qiskit logo on top
  • A Bloch sphere picture that never affects play
